RPG Stations 1.2.0 takes the new look CommonLib 3.0.0 brings to every menu, and a station that turns you away tells you why.

Built for Hytale Update 7, and needs Ziggfreed's CommonLib 3.0.0 or newer.

## Changes

- The station picker, which opens when what you put in a station fits more than one recipe, has the new look. Each recipe is a card with its cost, and past five and a half cards the list scrolls, so the whole picker fits on your screen.
- A station that turns you away says what you are missing.
- Bonuses from other mods that count only while you work at a station count at every RPG Stations station.

## For mod and pack authors

- A station's, an action's and a pattern's requirements are CommonLib's shared requirement block. `Conditions` still reads.
- A station's one action can carry settings for each player who works it, so several techniques combine on one station and a shared pile stays everyone's.
- An extension can say where its actions go in a station's list.
