Adds the second station, the Disenchanting Table. Place a piece of gear on the table and it comes apart over a staged ritual of about 15 seconds, giving back what the Salvage bench would plus Life Essence and the occasional find. You sit at the table the way you do at the Sawmill, free to look around with the mouse while your stand-in works. A four-piece disenchanter's kit, a grimoire and a draught each speed the ritual up, and with all of them it takes about 3 seconds. The Greater Disenchanting Table works three pieces in a row and pays more. Requires ZiggfreedCommon 2.2.0.
