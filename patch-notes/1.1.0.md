Adds the second station, the Disenchanting Table. Place a piece of gear on the table and it comes apart over a staged ritual of about 15 seconds, giving back what the Salvager's Workbench would plus Essence of Life and the occasional find. You sit at the table the way you do at the Sawmill, free to look around with the mouse while your stand-in works the ritual with empty hands. A four-piece disenchanter's kit, a grimoire and a draught each speed the ritual up, and with all of them it takes about 3 seconds. The Greater Disenchanting Table works three pieces in a row and pays more. Requires ZiggfreedCommon 2.2.0.

- The Sawmill's description says what the bench does, and that a better hatchet gets more planks
  out of each log. The key to press stays on the on-screen hint.
- Fixed: the work summary read "Worked 1 cycles" after a single cycle, and two stop messages mixed
  their tenses ("You moved away and stop working").
