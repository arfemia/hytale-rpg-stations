RPG Stations for Hytale Update 7. The stations work as before. The Disenchanting Table's bar shows how far the ritual has come, and the Disenchanter's Kit shows an icon beside your other effects. Requires Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.3.0.

- **Runs on Update 7 (0.7.x) servers.** Rebuilt for the new server line, so a 0.7 server loads it without the outdated-mod warning.
- **Needs Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.3.0, the library's Update 7 build.** With an older CommonLib installed, the server now refuses to load RPG Stations and names the library to update, rather than running it on a library that is quietly broken on Update 7.
- **No station warning for content made for a mod you don't have.** If a pack's stations post to a mod that isn't installed on your server, the station check that runs when the first player joins no longer warns about it. A misspelt channel of a mod you do have installed still gets its warning.
- **The Disenchanting Table's bar fills as the ritual goes.** While you disenchant, the table's bar on the left of your screen now fills a little with each stage of the ritual and is full as the ritual ends, where it used to sit full the whole time. On the Greater Disenchanting Table it fills once for each piece.
- **Added a Disenchanter's Kit icon beside your other effects.** Put on two pieces of the Disenchanter's Kit and one icon shows with your other effects, named for the most the kit is giving you: Disenchanter's Kit: Two Pieces, then Disenchanter's Kit: Full once all four are on, then Disenchanter's Kit: Full, Grimoire in Hand while you also hold the Disenchanter's Grimoire.
- **A clearer name in the emote list.** The gesture that begins a disenchanting ritual is now called Opening the Ritual. Your stand-in works with empty hands, so the old name, Opening the Grimoire, no longer fit.
