RPG Stations is built for Hytale Update 7 (0.7.x) and needs Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.3.0.

- Added a Disenchanter's Kit icon beside your other effects. Put on two pieces and it shows Disenchanter's Kit: Two Pieces. All four make it Disenchanter's Kit: Full, and holding the Disenchanter's Grimoire as well makes it Disenchanter's Kit: Full, Grimoire in Hand.
- The emote that starts a disenchanting ritual is now called Opening the Ritual. It was Opening the Grimoire.
- Fixed an issue where the Disenchanting Table's bar on the left of your screen sat full for the whole ritual. It now fills a little with each stage, and on the Greater Disenchanting Table it fills once for each piece.

### For server owners

- The station check that runs when the first player joins no longer warns about a pack's stations posting to a mod your server doesn't run. A misspelt channel of a mod you do run still gets its warning.
