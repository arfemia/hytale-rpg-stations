RPG Stations is built for Hytale Update 7 (0.7.x) and needs Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.3.0.

### Fixes

- Fixed the Greater Disenchanting Table asking for Bolts of Silk, which can't be made on Update 7. It now takes twelve Spider Silk, which cave spiders and Goblin Burners drop, with the Disenchanting Table, eight Thorium Ingots and twenty Essence of the Void as before.
