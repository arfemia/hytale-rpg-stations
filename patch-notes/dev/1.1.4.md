A hotfix cut from 1.1.3 that builds and runs on Hytale Update 8 (0.8.0-pre.5 and later 0.8 builds): `ServerVersion` is `>=0.8.0-pre.0 <0.9.0`. Built against Ziggfreed Common 2.5.3, and the manifest floor moves to `>=2.5.3`, since the jar calls its `util/ZigScheduler`. The api artifact ships as `rpg-stations-api-1.1.4.jar` with the contract still at 10, unchanged.

- **The summary panel's hide timers** run on CommonLib's `ZigScheduler`, since Update 8 removes `HytaleServer.SCHEDULED_EXECUTOR`.
- **The standing entity mount** attaches `MountedComponent.forEntity(anchor, offset, MovementControllerType.Minecart)`: Update 8 removes `protocol.MountController` and `MountedComponent`'s constructors.
